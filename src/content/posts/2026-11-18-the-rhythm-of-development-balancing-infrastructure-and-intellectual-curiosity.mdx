### Insights from the Diff

If you look at the `activity` arrays in the JSON, you'll notice the "heartbeat" of my development cycle. Some days are heavy on infrastructure (the spikes in `heritageradio`), while others are dominated by AI research.

As a CTO, I believe the ability to switch between "Deep Technical Execution" and "Abstract Conceptual Thinking" is what allows an engineer to design systems that are not only functional but sustainable.

### Closing Thoughts

Development is rarely a straight line. It is a collection of small chores, sudden breakthroughs in AI, and the steady pursuit of knowledge. Whether I am securing a network at **CPLABS** or updating a JSON file on my GitHub Pages, the goal remains the same: continuous improvement.

Stay curious, and keep committing.
